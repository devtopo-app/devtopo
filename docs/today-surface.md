# DevTopo - the "today" surface

The surface you glance at while working. Its job is to **orient and reassure**,
not to track you. The thing here no other tool can do: overlay live agent and
GitHub reality onto your own plan, across every project at once - without becoming
progress-police.

## What it shows

1. **A "state of now" line.** One calm, plain-language sentence orienting you
   ("Steady - two sessions running, one waiting on you, nothing on fire"). The one
   genuinely AI-generated element on the page. Orientation before detail.
2. **Each goal as a "planned vs what happened" tile.** For each thing you planned,
   one tile pairing the goal with what actually happened on it: a summary of the
   coding-agent context, your contributions and decisions, and the tangible outputs
   (commits, pushes, PRs). Rich and informative - intention overlaid with reality -
   but with no timing pressure: nothing about when it should have happened or hours
   spent vs intended.
3. **An "also happening" lane.** Live agent work that is not in the plan, shown
   neutrally - never as "deviation." Real days drift, and that is fine.
4. **Waiting on you.** Parked decisions, reviews requested of you, the snag. Gentle,
   pick-up-when-you-want, not an alarm.
5. **Contribution so far today.** What you moved forward, including invisible work
   (reviews, unblocking), plus the **AI working-hours** number ("Claude and Codex put
   in X hours today"). Feel-good framing, never compared to anyone. See
   `contribution-and-time.md` for how these are computed. Human / attention time is
   not shown (dropped product-wide).
6. **Light time awareness.** How much of the planning window is left - calendar /
   scheduling only, not an activity measure. Neutral and optional.

## Decisions

- **[Decided] We build the "today" surface** as the place a plan is reflected back
  while you work.
- **[Proposed] Works with or without a plan.** With no plan it still shows live
  reality and contribution. Planning is never required to get value - keeps "pull,
  not push" honest.
- **[Proposed] Reflection, not scorekeeping.** Plan-vs-reality shows as status and
  narrative, never "you are behind" or a burn-down against your plan. Off-plan work
  is "also did," not a miss.
- **[Decided] Rich on content, bare of time pressure.** Per-goal tiles pair "this was
  your goal" with "this is what happened" - agent-context summary, your contributions
  and decisions, and tangible outputs (commits, pushes, PRs). No when-it-should-happen
  and no time-spent-vs-intended framing. Informative, not a timesheet.
- **[Decided] Glance-only, no OS notifications.** You open it when you want. A
  passive in-app cue (a quiet dot when something is waiting) is fine, but nothing
  pings you outside the app. Keeps "pull, not push" intact.
- **[Proposed] AI understands, you decide.** It displays summaries and the single
  "state of now" sentence. No AI telling you what to do next.
- **[Proposed] Freshness is "as of the last checkpoint,"** not real-time. Be honest
  about the lag rather than implying live streaming.
- **[Decided] The today surface is the app's default landing screen.** It works with
  or without a plan, so it is always useful on open.
- **[Decided] It covers the whole day**, with current live session state emphasized
  on top. Contribution and AI-hours are day totals.
- **[Decided] Planner and today are separate views.** Planner is where you arrange
  intentions; today is where you watch them unfold.

## Principles carried from the product

- Buddy, not control center. Pressure down, never up. No comparison to others.
- The product's AI understands and summarizes; the human decides.
